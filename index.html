Translating Sacred Manuscripts into Woven Heritage

Jnana-Vastra bridges the ancient wisdom of calligraphic palm-leaf shlokas with state-of-the-art Electronic Jacquard technology. By transforming historic Sanskrit calligraphy into high-precision binary pixel weaves, we immortalize India's cultural heritage on sustainable, premium textiles.

The Innovation Behind Jnana-Vastra

Converting calligraphic scriptures directly into interlaced fabrics required solving multi-disciplinary challenges across textile CAD, warp tension, and bitmap drafting.

✍️

Manuscript Digitization

High-resolution scanning of traditional manuscripts, capturing subtle ink strokes, ligatures, and Sanskrit characters for vectorization.

🖩

CAD & 1-Bit Conversion

Exact mathematical reduction into clean 1-bit monochrome bitmaps where black and white pixels explicitly denote lift and drop sequences of Jacquard hooks.

⚙️

Electronic Jacquard Execution

Flawless execution on modern electronic Jacquard sheds, synchronizing pick selection to achieve high-contrast legibility.
