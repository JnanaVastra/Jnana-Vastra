Jnana-Vastra

Engineering Woven Heritage Manuscripts

Welcome to the digital archive accompanying the woven manuscript project. This portal connects the physical woven work with its digital manuscript, project documentation, authenticity information and audio resources.

Aim, Vision & Mission

Aim: To pioneer textile typography by converting ancient scriptural documentation into permanent structural jacquard woven books.
Vision: To transition traditional paper scripts into high-definition textile archives designed to endure across generations.
Mission: To execute precise CAD step-bindings, pick-and-pick weft insertion and reinforced book-page structures.
